Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Scrollength ("we", "our", or "the app") is a wellness app that helps you understand your social media scrolling habits by measuring the physical distance you scroll each day. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data collected on your device

Scrollength uses Android's UsageStatsManager API to measure the time you spend in social media apps (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat). This data is used solely to calculate your scroll distance and is processed entirely on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party.

2. Data you provide to us

If you choose to register for early access to Scrollength Pro, we collect your email address. This is the only personal data we store on our servers. We use your email address to:

  • Notify you when Scrollength Pro becomes available
  • Send you information about your early adopter discount

We will never sell your email address or use it for unrelated marketing.

3. Third-party services

Email addresses are stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Supabase processes data in accordance with GDPR. No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used in the app.

4. Data retention

Your email address is retained until you request its deletion or until it is no longer needed for the purposes described above.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at scrollength.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

Scrollength is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
