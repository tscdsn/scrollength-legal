Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Scrollength ("we", "our", or "the app") is a wellness application that helps you understand your social media scrolling habits by measuring the physical distance you scroll each day. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and your rights under applicable privacy laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (ZZPL).

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is the individual developer of Scrollength. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at: scrollength.app@gmail.com

2. Data collected on your device

Scrollength uses Android's UsageStatsManager API to measure the time you spend in social media apps (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat). This data is used solely to calculate your scroll distance and is processed entirely on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party. This data is stored locally in your device's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Data you provide to us

If you choose to register for early access to Scrollength Pro, we collect your email address. This is the only personal data we store on our servers. We use your email address solely to:

  • Notify you when Scrollength Pro becomes available
  • Send you information about your early adopter discount

We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, which you provide voluntarily when you choose to register for early access. You may withdraw your consent at any time by requesting deletion of your data (see Section 8). Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Third-party services

Email addresses are stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), ensuring compliance with GDPR data residency requirements. Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and processes data only according to our instructions.

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used in the app. We do not serve advertisements.

6. Data security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) on our database, preventing unauthorized access to your email address by other users
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the app and our servers
  • Restricted database access — only the app can write data; no public read access is permitted

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Data retention

Your email address is retained until you request its deletion or until Scrollength Pro is fully launched and you have had the opportunity to claim your early adopter discount, after which we will delete addresses of users who have not converted to paid subscribers within a reasonable time. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8).

8. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable Serbian law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — you may request deletion of your personal data at any time
  • Right to withdraw consent — you may withdraw your consent to processing at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (www.poverenik.rs) or with your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at scrollength.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

Scrollength is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Serbia, including the Law on Personal Data Protection (Official Gazette of the Republic of Serbia, No. 87/2018). For users in the European Union, the GDPR applies to the extent it is applicable.

12. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:
scrollength.app@gmail.com
