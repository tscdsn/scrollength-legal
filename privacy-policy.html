Privacy Policy

Scrollength · Last updated: 12 August 2026

In short: Scrollength works entirely on your device. We do not run any server, we do not have a database, we do not ask for an account or email, and we never receive or store your usage data. Your information stays with you.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Scrollength mobile application (the “App”) handles information. It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (“Zakon o zaštiti podataka o ličnosti”, Official Gazette No. 87/2018).

2. The data the App uses — and where it stays

To calculate how far you scroll, the App reads certain information locally on your device. This information is processed on the device and is never transmitted to the Developer or to any server we control:

  • App usage time for a fixed set of social apps (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat), read through Android’s Usage Access system, used to estimate scroll distance.
  • Your device’s screen dimensions, used together with usage time to estimate distance.
  • The distances the App calculates (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly totals and per-app breakdowns), stored locally so your history is available over time.

All of the above is stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. We do not see it, collect it, or upload it.

Scroll distance is an estimate based on time spent in an app, not a measurement of actual scrolling. It is intended for personal awareness only.

3. Permissions

The App requests one sensitive permission:

  • Usage Access — required by Android to read how long the tracked apps are used, which is the basis for the distance estimate. Without it, the App cannot function. This data is read and processed on the device only.

4. Local storage and device backup

Your data is kept in the App’s local storage on your device. One exception to “on-device only” is worth stating clearly:

  • Android backup. If you have Android’s built-in backup enabled, the operating system may store an encrypted copy of the App’s data in your own Google account (Google Drive), so your history can be restored if you reinstall the App or switch phones. This is a standard Android feature that you control in your device settings. The backup belongs to you and your Google account — the Developer has no access to it. It is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Scrollength offers an optional paid subscription (Scrollength Pro). If you choose to subscribe:

  • Google Play processes the payment. The App never sees or stores your card details or payment information. Payment handling is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat is used to manage your subscription status (for example, to confirm whether your subscription is active so the App can unlock paid features). For this purpose RevenueCat processes purchase-related identifiers and subscription information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Google Play account.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server or database that stores your data.
  • We do not require an account, a login, or your email address.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not run analytics that identify you.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly process any data from children under 13. Because the App collects no personal data on a server, it holds no such data in any case.

8. Data retention

Because your data lives on your device, it is retained for as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device. Any copy held in your own Android backup is controlled and removable through your Google account and device settings.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection regarding personal data. Since the Developer does not collect or hold your personal data on any server, there is generally no personal data on our side to access, correct, or delete — your data is under your own control on your device. For any question about these rights, contact us at the email above.

If you are in Serbia and believe your data protection rights have been violated, you may lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Poverenik): www.poverenik.rs. If you are in the EU/EEA, you may contact your local supervisory authority.

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Serbia, including the Law on Personal Data Protection (No. 87/2018), and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
