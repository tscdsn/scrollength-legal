Scrollength

Privacy Policy

Scrollength works on your phone. Your screen time and the distances it calculates never leave it, and the developer cannot see them.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

  • The app reads how long you spend in eight social apps, and the screen height you confirm during setup.
  • Everything is calculated and stored on your device, in the app's own private storage.
  • There is no account and no advertising.
  • The only information that leaves your phone is the record of a purchase, if you buy Pro. See What goes over the network.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Copies that Android keeps in your own Google account are described under Backups.

1. Who is responsible

Scrollength is made and published by an individual developer, who is responsible for the small amount of personal data described in this policy:

  • Name: Dušan Tošić
  • Based in: Serbia
  • Email: scrollength.app@gmail.com

No screen time, distance or usage information ever reaches the developer. The only thing the developer can see is the record of a purchase, under a random identifier, as described in Purchases.

2. What the app reads

Usage time

With your permission (Android's Usage Access), Scrollength reads how long the following apps have been in the foreground on your device:

  • TikTok
  • Instagram
  • Facebook
  • Snapchat
  • Reddit
  • X
  • Pinterest
  • Threads

The app reads only the amount of time, and only for these apps. It cannot see, and does not read, what you looked at, who you spoke to, what you typed, or anything inside those apps. It has no access to your messages, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Your screen height

The app measures the height of your screen and asks you to confirm it during setup. This number is used to convert time into an estimated distance. It is a measurement of your phone, not of you.

App names and icons

To show which app a number belongs to, Scrollength asks Android for the name and icon of apps already installed on your device. These come from the system, are used only for display, and are never sent anywhere.

3. What the app stores

All of the following is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • A daily log of estimated distance, overall and per app
  • Today's running total
  • Your chosen unit (meters or feet) and your screen height
  • Your daily limit, if you set one, and the date you set it
  • Whether setup is complete, the date the app was last opened, and the date it was installed
  • Whether you have Pro, and whether it came from a one-time purchase or a subscription
  • Which milestone cards you have already been shown today, so the same one does not appear twice
  • A random identifier created by the purchase component (RevenueCat), and its last known copy of your purchase status

There is no name, email address or phone number in this list, and the app does not use an advertising ID.

4. What goes over the network

Your screen time, the distances, your history, the names of your apps and your screen height are never sent anywhere. They exist only on your phone.

The app uses the network for one thing: Pro. It contacts Google Play and RevenueCat (see Purchases) when the app starts or comes back to the screen, when you open the Pro screen to see prices, when you buy, and when you restore a purchase. Without a connection the app keeps working; only buying and restoring need one.

A few buttons — this policy, the terms of service and subscription management — open your web browser. That is your browser making the request, not the app.

When you use Export my data or share a card, the app hands the file or picture to Android's share menu, and you choose where it goes. The app itself sends it nowhere.

5. Backups

Android offers its own backup service. If you have it switched on in your device settings, Android may copy the app's stored data into your own Google account, so it can be restored if you change phones. This is a feature of Android, not of Scrollength. The copy belongs to you, is covered by Google's terms, and the developer has no access to it.

You can turn Android backup off in your device settings, usually under System → Backup.

So that a purchase can be recognised after you reinstall the app, the RevenueCat component may also keep its random identifier in Google's Block Store, a small storage area provided by Google Play services that can keep it when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. It holds only that identifier, never your screen time.

6. Purchases

Scrollength offers an optional upgrade to Pro, either as a one-time purchase or as a monthly subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees or receives your card number, bank details or billing address. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases are confirmed through RevenueCat, a service that sits between the app and Google Play and works on the developer's behalf. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated identifier for your copy of the app, the history of what you bought and when, the country and currency of your store, and basic technical details such as the app version, the Android version and an IP address. The random identifier is not linked to your name or email, and none of your screen time or distances are ever sent to it.

RevenueCat also uses these purchase records to show the developer statistics, such as how many people bought Pro and when. This is the only analytics Scrollength uses. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to the developer or to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat, Inc. is based in the United States and processes this information there. Transfers from Europe rely on the standard contractual clauses that RevenueCat provides in its data processing terms. RevenueCat's own handling of this information is covered by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7. Legal basis

The only personal data the developer handles is the purchase record described above. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act, it is handled on these grounds:

  • Confirming and restoring a purchase is necessary to give you what you paid for (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Purchase statistics rely on the developer's legitimate interest in knowing how Pro sells (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by writing to the email above.

Your screen time is not handled by the developer at all. It is calculated and kept only on your phone. Scrollength makes no automated decisions about you and builds no profile of you.

8. How long data is kept

Everything on your phone stays there until you remove it. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app, which deletes everything it stored on the phone.

The record of a purchase is kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as it is needed to give you access to what you bought, including restoring it on a new phone, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act, you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

Because almost everything stays on your device, most of these you can exercise directly, without asking anyone:

  • Access and portability — Settings → Export my data writes a CSV file of every day the app has recorded, which you can open in any spreadsheet.
  • Erasure — clear the app's storage, or uninstall it.
  • Correction — your unit, screen height and daily limit can all be changed in Settings.

For your purchase record, write to the email above and the developer will have it deleted, or answer any other request about it. You will get a reply within one month. Deleting the record may mean a purchase can no longer be restored automatically.

If you believe your rights have been infringed you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Serbia this is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection. In the EU it is the data protection authority of the country you live in.

10. Children

Scrollength is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect anything from them.
