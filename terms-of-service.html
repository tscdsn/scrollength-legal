4. The numbers are estimates, not measurements

This is the most important thing on this page, so it gets its own box.

Scrollength does not measure your thumb. It cannot see your finger, your gestures, or anything that happens inside another app. What it actually does is take the time an app spent in the foreground, multiply it by your screen height, and multiply that by an assumed rate of scrolling for that particular app.

That assumed rate is an average. Your real behaviour will differ from it — possibly by a lot. If you leave an app open without touching it, that time still counts. If you scroll far faster or slower than most people, the figure will be wrong in that direction.

Distances in the app are marked with the symbol ≈ for this reason. Treat the figures as a rough indication of scale, useful for comparing one week with another, and not as a measurement of anything.

We make no promise that any number is accurate, and we are not liable for decisions you make based on them.
